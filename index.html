Lowest entry

1405 is the cheapest at AED 19 M, but it is also the smallest unit and has the smallest terrace (29 sqm).

Best terrace

1301 has an 88 sqm terrace and is fully furnished at 9,672 AED / sqft, the lowest rate among the upgraded units.

Lowest AED / sqft

1201 is the largest unit at the lowest rate (9,495), but its interior is Standard and it has the highest ticket (AED 28 M). Allow for fit-out.

Most expensive per sqft

901 carries the highest rate (11,050) even after the largest discount (17%). It only competes if the price moves further.
